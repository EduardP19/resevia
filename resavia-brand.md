# Resavia — Brand Document

> **Every call answered. Every booking captured.**

---

## What Is Resavia?

Resavia is an AI receptionist built for beauty salons, aesthetic clinics, and dental practices. It handles the front-desk work that slips through the cracks — missed calls, out-of-hours enquiries, and appointment bookings — so owners never lose a client to an unanswered phone again.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Small clinics and salons are run by busy, skilled people. When they're with a client, they can't answer the phone. When they're closed, enquiries go nowhere. The result:

- Missed calls = missed bookings = lost revenue
- Hiring a receptionist is expensive and often overkill for a small team

Resavia fixes both.

---

## What Resavia Does (V1)

| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Missed call recovery** | When a call goes unanswered, Resavia automatically sends an SMS to the caller to re-engage them |
| **Out-of-hours booking** | Clients can book appointments via SMS conversation, any time of day |
| **FAQ handling** | Resavia answers common questions (services, prices, opening hours) pulled from the salon's own database |
| **Appointment reminders** | Automated reminders sent to clients ahead of their booking to reduce no-shows |

---

## Who It's For

**Primary audience:** Owners and managers of:
- Beauty salons
- Aesthetic clinics
- Dental practices

**Profile:** Solo operators or small teams (1–10 staff) who rely on phone and walk-in bookings, don't have a dedicated receptionist, and are losing revenue to missed or unanswered calls.

---

## Brand Personality

Resavia is **premium and sleek** — not corporate, not chatty. The brand feels like the tool itself: quiet, efficient, always on.

| Trait | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Premium** | Clean visuals, confident language, no fluff |
| **Sleek** | Minimal design, short copy, nothing unnecessary |
| **Reliable** | Always available, always professional |
| **Empowering** | Gives small business owners the power of a full front desk |

**Tone of voice:** Calm. Direct. Professional. Speaks like a high-end service, not a startup.

Use these as the foundation for all marketing assets:
